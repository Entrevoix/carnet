Carnet is a mobile-first capture tool for people who keep their notes as plain
Markdown files in an Obsidian vault.

There is no server and no database. Carnet writes ordinary Markdown files
straight into a folder on your device — the same folder your Syncthing app
watches — and Syncthing carries them to your workstation and into your vault.
Carnet never talks to a sync service of its own; Syncthing (which you already
run and control) does that job. No account, no sign-up, no cloud storage.

Five capture modes cover the way notes actually happen:
* Idea — type or dictate a quick note
* Journal — voice or text, appended to today's daily note
* Person — scan a business card (on-device OCR) and dictate context
* Photo — snap a picture, paired with its own note
* Audio — record a voice memo, transcribed on-device

Captures also arrive through Android's share sheet (share a link, image, or
text straight into Carnet from any app) and from a persistent notification's
inline reply, so a thought never needs its own app-switch.

LLM enrichment (tagging, cleanup, structure) is entirely optional and
entirely yours to configure: point Carnet at a self-hosted OmniRoute
instance, a local on-device runtime (Relais on the same handset), or any
OpenAI-compatible endpoint you already run or trust. Every capture is
save-first — the Markdown file lands on disk immediately, and enrichment
patches it afterward — so a flaky network or a slow model never costs you a
note. Anything captured offline queues on-device and drains automatically
once you're back online.

Notes stay browsable and searchable right in the app: full text, tags, and a
recent-captures list, all reading from the same files Obsidian sees.

Carnet has no analytics and no trackers of any kind. The only network calls
it makes are the ones you configure yourself (your LLM endpoint, and,
optionally, a self-hosted Karakeep instance for one-tap bookmark export).
API keys for those live in the Android Keystore-backed secure storage, never
in plain text.

Carnet is Android-only today — there is no iOS build.

Carnet is free and open source software, licensed AGPL-3.0-only.
